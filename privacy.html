1. Who we are

Toastid Tech, LLC ("Toastid Tech," "we," "us") is an Arkansas company based in Van Buren, Arkansas. We operate toastidtech.com, tryravuna.com, hestrel.toastidtech.com, and our other product sites and apps, including Ravuna (AI receptionist) and Hestrel (consent-first estimating). This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Questions: info@toastidtech.com or (479) 339-1504.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and business information: business name, contact name, email, phone number, hours, services, service area, booking rules, and similar setup details.
  • Messages you send us through contact forms, email, or phone.
  • Payment information is collected and processed by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Information collected when you use Ravuna (as a caller or a customer business)

  • Call audio, recordings, and transcripts of calls to a Ravuna-powered business line.
  • Caller name, phone number, and the details a caller shares to book an appointment or request a callback.
  • Call logs: date, time, duration, and outcome of each call.
  • This information belongs to the business whose line was called and is made available to that business.

Information collected when you use Hestrel

  • Business profile, pricing, terms, and payment link the professional enters during setup.
  • Estimate content: job details, line items, dictated notes (after signed consent), and photos the professional attaches.
  • Customer signatures: the consent signature captured before dictation begins and the approval signature captured when work is approved, stored as part of the job packet.
  • Hestrel is designed to store working data on the professional's own device first.

Information collected automatically

  • Website analytics (pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type) through our analytics and advertising measurement tools, including Google Analytics, the Meta Pixel, and the TikTok Pixel.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used for site function, analytics, and measuring our advertising.
  • Basic server logs (IP address, timestamps, error logs) for security and reliability.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the services: answer and log calls, book appointments, generate summaries and transcripts, build estimate packets, and run your account.
  • To communicate with you: service notices, support replies, billing, and product updates.
  • To improve and secure the services: troubleshooting, quality review, and preventing abuse.
  • To measure our marketing (in aggregate) through the analytics tools listed above.
  • To comply with the law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your business data or your customers' call content to train public AI models.

4. Call recording notice

Calls to a Ravuna-powered line may be recorded and transcribed so the business can receive accurate summaries and logs. Ravuna answers in the business's name and identifies the business she is answering for. Businesses using Ravuna are responsible for the caller disclosures and consents required by the laws that apply to them; we configure Ravuna's setup to support those disclosures.

5. How we share information

  • With the business you called or hired: if you call a business that uses Ravuna, your call details go to that business — that is the point of the service. If a professional uses Hestrel on your job, your signed consent, approval, and estimate packet are part of that professional's job record.
  • With service providers who help us run the services — telephony, speech-to-text, AI processing, cloud hosting, email, analytics, and payment processing — under agreements that limit their use of the data to providing their service.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the services.
  • Business transfers: if Toastid Tech, LLC is sold or merged, information may transfer with the business, subject to this policy.

6. Data retention

Call recordings, transcripts, and logs are retained for as long as the customer business's account is active or as needed to provide the service, unless the business asks us to delete them sooner. Estimate packets are retained by the professional using Hestrel. Contact-form messages and support emails are retained for as long as needed to handle the conversation and keep business records. Analytics data is retained according to the analytics providers' standard settings.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing info@toastidtech.com. We respond to verified requests within a reasonable time.
  • If you are a caller and want a recording or transcript of your call deleted, contact the business you called, or contact us and we will route the request.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser; some site features may work less well.
  • You can opt out of marketing emails with the unsubscribe link in any marketing email we send. Service and billing notices are not marketing and cannot be opted out of while you have an account.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law; we honor verified requests as described above regardless of state.

8. Security

We use reputable cloud providers, encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege practices to protect information. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. They are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Cope, our wellness companion, is intended for adults. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links
