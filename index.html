FAQ

Straight answers

What happens when Ravuna can't answer something?

She doesn't guess. If a caller asks something outside what she's been set up with, Ravuna takes their name and number and books a callback, so you or your team can follow up with the right answer.

Can she transfer a caller to me live?

Yes. When a call needs you — a regular customer, an urgent job, anyone you'd rather handle personally — Ravuna can transfer the caller straight through to you while they're on the line.

What do I need to get started?

Just your business basics: what you do, your hours, the services you offer, and how you like calls handled. We build Ravuna around that and white-label her to your business — she answers in your name, not ours.

How long until she's live on my line?

About two weeks from setup to live. She's built, tested, and tuned on your business information before she ever answers a real caller.

Is my business information private?

Yes. Ravuna is set up for your business alone, answers using your business's information, and the details from your calls are logged to your CRM — your callers' information goes to you.
